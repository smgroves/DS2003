Project 1 Presentation Rubric

Component Excellent Satisfactory Poor
Insight Selection Chose the most compelling finding(s) from the analysis; clearly prioritized rather than trying to cover everything. Covered reasonable content, but didn't clearly prioritize the strongest insight over weaker ones. Presented trivial or arbitrary content with no sense of what mattered most.
Visual Communication Walked the audience through each chart's encoding and what to notice; the audience could follow the insight without having read the report. Explained charts adequately, but leaned on captions or assumed background the audience didn't have. Showed charts without explaining them, or the audience couldn't tell what the chart was showing.
Structure & Clarity Clear beginning (context/question), middle (evidence), and end (takeaway); easy to follow start to finish. Generally organized, but some transitions were unclear or the throughline was hard to follow. Disorganized or rambling; no clear structure.
Time Management Finished within the 5-minute limit with time used purposefully, no rushing or padding. Ran slightly over/under, or paced unevenly (e.g., rushed the ending). Significantly over/under time, or clearly unrehearsed.
Delivery & Engagement Confident, audience-facing delivery; engaged the room rather than reading off the screen. Adequate delivery, but relied heavily on reading from notes/screen. Difficult to follow due to delivery (e.g., inaudible, entirely read from a script).
