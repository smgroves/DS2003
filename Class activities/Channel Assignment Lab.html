An activity for DS 2003 — Communicating with Data. Drag 5 of these attributes onto the 5 channels, pick a chart type, and see how close you get to the theoretical best.

Attribute pool

60 fictional hiking trails (illustrative data, not a real dataset), 8 attributes. Drag exactly 5 onto the channels below — the rest are fine to leave unused.

Channels

Drop an attribute onto a channel. Dropping onto an occupied channel sends the previous occupant back to the pool.

Chart type

Different chart types imply different channel jobs — a bar's length only means something if one axis is categorical and the other is a quantity.
